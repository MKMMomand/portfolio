About

The short version

I've spent 11 years moving between the ledger, the factory floor, and the code editor. I started in hospitality accounting, grew into operations leadership in manufacturing and distribution, and later added certified software development in Python and Django. That combination is unusual — and it's exactly why I'm useful.

In practice, I've managed procurement departments, planned production schedules, supervised technical staff, prepared VAT returns, and led general operations for a herbal pharma manufacturer. Alongside that, I build tools: I'm currently developing Binyad Office, an office management platform to replace manual paperwork in small businesses.

I work across cultures and languages — English, Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Persian, Pashto, and Dari — and I'm comfortable being the person who connects finance, operations, and technology into one coherent picture.
